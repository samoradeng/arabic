## 2. Formal vs. Informal Register

Jordanians switch between registers depending on context:

| Situation | Register | Example "How are you?" |
|-----------|----------|----------------------|
| Friends | Informal | كيفك يا زلمة؟ |
| Workplace | Semi-formal | كيف حالك؟ |
| Official | Formal | كيف حالكم؟ (using plural for respect) |

## 3. Work Dialogue — Job Interview

> — أهلا وسهلا. تفضل اقعد.
> — ahla w sahla. tfa99al u23ud.
> — *Welcome. Please sit down.*

> — شكرا.
> — shukran.

> — طيب، خبرني عن حالك. شو درست؟
> — 6ayyib, khabbirni 3an 7aalak. shu darast?
> — *OK, tell me about yourself. What did you study?*

> — درست هندسة كمبيوتر بالجامعة الأردنية. تخرجت سنة ألفين وعشرين.
> — darast handase kompyooter bil-jaam3a il-urduniyye. takharrajt sane alfein w 3ishreen.
> — *I studied computer engineering at the University of Jordan. I graduated in 2020.*

> — وشو خبرتك؟
> — w shu khibirtak?
> — *And what's your experience?*

> — اشتغلت سنتين بشركة برمجة. كنت مبرمج. وبعدين اشتغلت بشركة ثانية كمدير مشروع.
> — ishtazhalt sanatein b-sharikit barmaje. kunt mubarmi. w ba3dein ishtazhalt b-sharike taanye ka-mudeer mashroo3.
> — *I worked two years at a programming company. I was a programmer. Then I worked at another company as a project manager.*

> — منيح. شو مهاراتك الأساسية؟
> — mnee7. shu mahaaratak il-asaasiyye?
> — *Good. What are your main skills?*

> — بحكي إنجليزي وعربي. عندي خبرة بالبرمجة وإدارة المشاريع. وبحب أشتغل بفريق.
> — ba7ki ingleezi w 3arabi. 3indi khibre bil-barmaje w idaarit il-mashaaree3. w ba7ibb ashtaghil b-faree2.
> — *I speak English and Arabic. I have experience in programming and project management. And I like working in a team.*

> — كم بتتوقع الراتب؟
> — kam btitawwa2a3 ir-raatib?
> — *What salary do you expect?*

> — حسب الوظيفة والمسؤوليات. بس بتوقع شي منافس بالسوق.
> — 7asab il-wa6eefe wil-mas2ooliyyaat. bass btawwa2a3 shi munaafis bis-soo2.
> — *Depending on the position and responsibilities. But I expect something competitive in the market.*

## 5. Exercises

### Exercise A: Describe your work/studies in Arabic (8+ sentences)
Include: what you do, where, how long, what you like/dislike about it.

### Exercise B: Write a formal request (in Arabic)
You want to ask your manager for a week off next month. Write 4-5 formal sentences.

### Exercise C: Translate
1. I've been working at this company for 3 years.
2. The meeting is at 10 tomorrow morning.
3. What's your experience in this field?
4. I want to apply for the manager position.

---

### Answer Key

**Exercise C:**
1. بشتغل بهالشركة من ثلاث سنين — bashtaghil b-hash-sharike min thalath sineen
2. الاجتماع بكرة الساعة عشرة الصبح — il-ijtimaa3 bukra is-saa3a 3ashara i9-9ub7
3. شو خبرتك بهالمجال؟ — shu khibirtak b-hal-majaal?
4. بدي أقدم على وظيفة المدير — biddi a2addim 3ala wa6eefit il-mudeer
