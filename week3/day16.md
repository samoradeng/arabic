## 1. Essential Jordanian Slang

These are words you'll hear constantly that aren't in any textbook:

| Slang | Transliteration | Meaning | Context |
|-------|----------------|---------|---------|
| يا زلمة | ya zalame | dude / man | Casual address |
| يا معلم | ya m3allim | boss / master | To shopkeepers, drivers |
| يا عمي | ya 3ammi | uncle / bro | Friendly address to anyone |
| فش | fish | there isn't | Short for ما في شي |
| هسا / هلأ | hassa / halla2 | now / right now | هسا is more Jordanian |
| عادي | 3aadi | normal / no big deal | Response to apologies |
| زي الزفت | zay iz-zift | like tar = terrible | The weather is zay iz-zift |
| مية بالمية | miyye bil-miyye | 100% / definitely | |
| الله يسترها | allah yistirha | God protect us | When worried |
| طز | 6uzz | screw it / I don't care | Rude but common |
| خلص | khala9 | done / enough / stop | Very versatile |
| أخ! | akh! | ouch / ugh | Pain or frustration |
| تكرم عينك | tikram 3einak | "your eye is honored" | = of course / gladly |
| ولك | walak | hey you (m) | Casual, between close friends |
| فلتة | falte | genius / one-of-a-kind | Compliment (slightly joking) |
| مخّه وسّع | mukhkhu wassa3 | his brain expanded | = he's very smart |

## 4. Humor — How Jordanians Joke

### Self-deprecating humor
Jordanians love making fun of themselves and their country's quirks:
- Traffic (الأزمة), bureaucracy (الروتين), and family expectations

### Exaggeration
- والله لو إنه آخر شي بسويه بحياتي — wallah law innu aakhir shi bsawweehi b-7ayaati — I swear even if it's the last thing I do in my life

### Wordplay
- Many jokes play on double meanings of Arabic words

### Sarcasm
- إن شاء الله (inshaallah) said flat = "that's never happening"
- بكرة (bukra - tomorrow) = "never"

### Sample Jordanian Joke Structure

> واحد أردني راح على المطعم. سأل: "في منسف؟"
> الزلمة قاله: "لا، خلص."
> قاله: "طيب أنا رح أستنى لما يصير في."
> قعد ثلاث ساعات!

> *A Jordanian went to a restaurant. He asked: "Is there mansaf?"*
> *The guy said: "No, it's finished."*
> *He said: "OK, I'll wait until there is."*
> *He sat for three hours!*

(The joke: Jordanians won't settle for anything but mansaf.)

## 5. Slang in Context — Dialogue

> — يا زلمة وينك من زمان؟! ما شفتك!
> — ya zalame weinak min zamaan?! maa shuftak!
> — *Dude, where have you been?! I haven't seen you!*

> — والله يا عمي كنت مشغول زي الزفت. شغل وجامعة. ما إلي خلق.
> — wallah ya 3ammi kunt mashghool zay iz-zift. shughul w jaam3a. maa ili khul2.
> — *I swear, uncle, I was terribly busy. Work and university. I had no energy.*

> — مسكين! طيب هسا فاضي؟ يلا نتعشى.
> — miskeen! 6ayyib hassa faa9i? yalla nit3asha.
> — *Poor guy! OK, you free now? Let's have dinner.*

> — تكرم عينك! وين؟
> — tikram 3einak! wein?
> — *Of course! Where?*

> — في مطعم جديد جنب الدوار. أكلهم مية بالمية.
> — fii ma63am jdeed janb id-dawwaar. aklhum miyye bil-miyye.
> — *There's a new restaurant by the roundabout. Their food is 100%.*

> — يلا يلا! بس ادفع إنت لأنه أنا مفلس! ههه
> — yalla yalla! bass idfa3 inte la2innu ana muflis! hhh
> — *Let's go! But you pay because I'm broke! Hahaha*
