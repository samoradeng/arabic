# Day 15: Conditionals & Hypotheticals

## Lesson Goals
- Use "if" statements (إذا / لو)
- Express hypothetical situations
- Use لازم (must), ممكن (possible), لازم (should)
- Handle complex sentence structures

---

## 1. Real Conditionals — إذا (idha) — "If"

For things that could actually happen:

**إذا + past/present verb, رح + verb**

| Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning |
|--------|----------------|---------|
| إذا بدك، تعال | idha biddak, ta3aal | If you want, come |
| إذا في وقت، رح أزورك | idha fii wa2t, ra7 azuurak | If there's time, I'll visit you |
| إذا ما اجا، بنروح بدونه | idha maa ija, binroo7 bidoonu | If he doesn't come, we'll go without him |
| إذا الجو حلو، رح نطلع | idha ij-jaww 7ilu, ra7 nu6la3 | If the weather is nice, we'll go out |

---

## 2. Hypothetical/Unreal — لو (law) — "If (but unlikely)"

For wishes, regrets, and unreal situations:

**لو + past verb, كان + past verb**

| Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning |
|--------|----------------|---------|
| لو كنت بعرف، كنت قلتلك | law kunt ba3rif, kunt 2ultillak | If I had known, I would have told you |
| لو عندي فلوس، كنت سافرت | law 3indi floos, kunt saafart | If I had money, I would have traveled |
| لو كنت أردني، كنت أحكي أحسن | law kunt urduni, kunt a7ki a7san | If I were Jordanian, I'd speak better |

### يا ريت (ya reit) — "I wish"

- يا ريت بعرف عربي أحسن — ya reit ba3rif 3arabi a7san — I wish I knew Arabic better
- يا ريت كنت هناك — ya reit kunt hunaak — I wish I had been there
- يا ريتني ما أكلت كتير — ya reitni maa akalt kteer — I wish I hadn't eaten so much

## 3. Modals — Must, Can, Should

| Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning | Example |
|--------|----------------|---------|---------|
| لازم | laazim | must / have to | لازم أروح — I have to go |
| مش لازم | mish laazim | don't have to | مش لازم تيجي — You don't have to come |
| ممكن | mumkin | possible / can | ممكن أسألك؟ — Can I ask you? |
| مش ممكن | mish mumkin | impossible | مش ممكن! — Impossible! |
| بقدر | ba2dar | I can / able to | بقدر أساعدك — I can help you |
| ما بقدر | maa ba2dar | I can't | ما بقدر أيجي — I can't come |
| المفروض | il-mafroo9 | supposed to / should | المفروض تدرس — You should study |

## 4. Conditional Dialogue

> — إذا خلصت شغل بدري بكرة، شو بدك تسوي؟
> — idha khala9t shughul badri bukra, shu biddak tsawwi?
> — *If you finish work early tomorrow, what will you do?*

> — إذا خلصت بدري، رح أروح أتمرن بالجم.
> — idha khala9t badri, ra7 aroo7 atmarran bil-gym.
> — *If I finish early, I'll go work out at the gym.*

> — ولو ما خلصت بدري؟
> — w law maa khala9t badri?
> — *And if you don't finish early?*

> — يعني... رح أضطر أضل بالمكتب. بس إن شاء الله بخلص.
> — ya3ni... ra7 a96arr a9all bil-maktab. bass inshaallah bakhla9.
> — *Well... I'll have to stay at the office. But God willing I'll finish.*

> — لو كنت مكانك، كنت حكيت مع المدير.
> — law kunt makaanak, kunt 7akeet ma3 il-mudeer.
> — *If I were you, I would've talked to the manager.*

> — يمكن لازم أحكي معه. معك حق.
> — yimkin laazim a7ki ma3u. ma3ak 7a22.
> — *Maybe I should talk to him. You're right.*

## 5. Common Conditional Expressions

| Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning |
|--------|----------------|---------|
| لو سمحت | law sama7t | if you please (= please) |
| إذا الله راد | idha allah raad | if God wills |
| لو كنت مكانك | law kunt makaanak | if I were you |
| على حسب | 3ala 7asab | depending on |
| مهما صار | mahma 9aar | no matter what happens |
| حتى لو | 7atta law | even if |

## 6. Exercises

### Exercise A: Complete with إذا or لو
1. ___ بدك تنجح، لازم تدرس. (If you want to succeed, you must study)
2. ___ كنت غني، كنت اشتريت بيت كبير. (If I were rich, I'd buy a big house)
3. ___ الجو حار، مش رح نطلع. (If the weather is hot, we won't go out)
4. ___ عرفت من قبل، كنت ما رحت. (If I had known earlier, I wouldn't have gone)

### Exercise B: Express these using modals
1. I have to study tonight.
2. I can't come tomorrow.
3. You should eat before the medicine.
4. Is it possible to sit here?

### Exercise C: Write 5 "I wish" sentences using يا ريت
Think about real wishes related to your Arabic learning journey.
