## Final Fluency Assessment

### Part 1: Quick-Fire Translation (20 points)

Translate these to Jordanian Arabic as fast as you can:

1. Hi, how are you? I'm fine, thank God.
2. I have 3 brothers and 2 sisters. My older brother is married.
3. It's 4:30. The shop closes at 10 at night.
4. I wake up every day at 7, shower, and go to work by car.
5. I want mansaf without onion and Arabic coffee without sugar.
6. Where is the nearest pharmacy? Go straight then turn left.
7. Yesterday I went to the market and bought a jacket. It was expensive.
8. This is better than that, but I prefer the blue one.
9. My head hurts and I have a fever. I need medicine.
10. I'm going to travel to Petra next week, God willing.

### Part 3: Extended Writing (25 points)

Write ONE of these (minimum 15 sentences):

**Option A:** Tell the story of your Arabic learning journey. What motivated you? What was hard? What did you enjoy?

**Option B:** You're visiting Amman for the first time. Describe your entire first day — from the airport to dinner to sleep.

**Option C:** Have a debate with an imaginary Jordanian friend about whether it's better to learn Arabic online or in Jordan. Present both sides.

### Part 4: Cultural Competency (20 points)

What do you say/do in these situations?

1. You enter a shop where the owner is arranging shelves.
2. Someone offers you coffee. You're not thirsty.
3. A friend tells you their grandmother died.
4. You see your friend's new baby.
5. You want to compliment the food at someone's house.
6. Someone asks كيفك — give a natural response with follow-up.
7. A taxi driver charges 10 dinars for a 3-dinar ride.
8. You want to end a phone call politely.
9. You're leaving someone's house after dinner.
10. Someone says "Your Arabic is amazing!"

### Part 5: Conversation Simulation (20 points)

Write a complete 20-line dialogue covering ALL of these:
- Meeting someone new
- Introductions and small talk
- Discussing what you do and where you're from
- Making plans to meet again
- Exchanging phone numbers

## Self-Assessment Rubric

### Speaking Ability

| Level | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **A1 — Breakthrough** | Can use basic greetings and introduce yourself |
| **A2 — Waystage** | Can handle simple daily transactions and small talk |
| **B1 — Threshold** | Can handle most situations while traveling in Jordan, express opinions, tell stories |
| **B2 — Vantage** | Can interact with natives fluently, discuss abstract topics, understand most conversations |

**If you completed this program diligently, you should be at solid A2 heading toward B1.** That means you can:
- Navigate any daily situation in Jordan
- Have real conversations with Jordanians
- Understand most of what people say to you (at a moderate pace)
- Express your thoughts, opinions, and stories
- Read menus, signs, and simple texts
