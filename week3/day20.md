## 1. Hospitality — الضيافة

Jordan has one of the strongest hospitality cultures on earth. Rules:

### The Hospitality Code

1. **Guests are sacred.** A guest (ضيف — 9eif) is treated like royalty.
2. **Always offer food/drink.** Not offering is shameful.
3. **Refuse first, accept second.** When offered something, politely decline once, then accept.
4. **Never visit empty-handed.** Bring sweets, fruit, or something.
5. **Remove shoes** at the door (unless told otherwise).
6. **Compliment the food** — say تسلم إيديك and صحتين.
7. **Stay for tea.** Leaving right after eating is rude.

### Hospitality Phrases

| ما بنقصر | maa bn2a99ir | We won't fall short (= we'll treat you well) |

## 2. Religious & Cultural Expressions

You don't need to be Muslim to use these — they're part of the language:

| Arabic | Transliteration | When to Use |
|--------|----------------|-------------|
| بسم الله | bismillah | Before eating, starting anything |
| الحمد لله | il-7amdu lillah | After eating, when asked how you are, expressing gratitude |
| إن شاء الله | inshaallah | Any future plan |
| ما شاء الله | mashaallah | Complimenting someone/something (prevents evil eye) |
| الله يرحمه/ها | allah yir7amu/ha | God rest his/her soul (about deceased) |
| الله يشفيه/ها | allah yishfeeh/ha | God heal him/her (about sick person) |
| البقاء لله | il-ba2aa2 lillah | "Permanence belongs to God" (condolences) |
| مبروك | mabrook | Congratulations |
| الله يبارك فيك | allah ybaarik feek | God bless you (response to mabrook) |
| الله يعطيك العافية | allah ya36eek il-3aafye | God give you strength |
| الله يوفقك | allah ywaffi2ak | God grant you success |
| رمضان كريم | rama9aan kareem | Ramadan is generous (Ramadan greeting) |
| عيد مبارك | 3eed mubaarak | Blessed holiday |
| الله يخليلك إياهم | allah ykhalleelak iyyaahum | God preserve them for you (about children) |

> **Important:** When someone mentions a dead relative, ALWAYS say الله يرحمه/ها. When someone is sick, ALWAYS say الله يشفيه/ها. These are expected, not optional.

## 3. Social Do's and Don'ts

### DO:
- **Use your right hand** for eating, giving, and receiving
- **Accept hospitality** — at least a small cup of coffee/tea
- **Ask about family** — it shows you care
- **Say mashaallah** when complimenting children (protects from evil eye)
- **Dress modestly** when visiting homes or rural areas
- **Stand when elders enter** the room

### DON'T:
- **Don't show the bottom of your feet** to people — it's insulting
- **Don't refuse food repeatedly** — it offends the host
- **Don't discuss politics** unless the other person brings it up
- **Don't criticize Islam** or religion in general
- **Don't be overly physical** with the opposite gender (handshake only, and some women won't shake hands — wait for them to extend first)
- **Don't photograph people** without asking

## 4. Navigating Common Social Situations

### Situation 1: Visiting a Jordanian Home

> *You arrive:*
> — السلام عليكم! (is-salaamu 3aleikum!)
> — وعليكم السلام! أهلا وسهلا! تفضل تفضل! (tfa99al tfa99al!)
>
> *They offer food:*
> — لا شكرا، ما تتعبو (first refusal)
> — لا لازم تاكل! عملنالك منسف!
> — طيب، تسلمو. بسم الله. (accept on second offer)
>
> *After eating:*
> — الحمد لله. الأكل كان زاكي كتير. تسلم إيديك يا أم أحمد.
> — صحتين! بدك شاي ولا قهوة؟
> — قهوة، لو سمحتي.

### Situation 2: Receiving a Compliment

In Arab culture, deflect compliments to avoid the evil eye:

> — ابنك شاطر كتير!
> — *Your son is very smart!*
> — ما شاء الله، الله يخليلي إياه.
> — *Mashallah, may God keep him for me.*

### Situation 3: Someone's Relative Passed Away

> — خالي توفى إمبارح.
> — *My uncle passed away yesterday.*
> — إنا لله وإنا إليه راجعون. الله يرحمه ويصبركم. البقاء لله.
> — *To God we belong and to Him we return. God have mercy on him and give you patience. Permanence belongs to God.*

## 5. Key Cultural Concepts

### الكرم (il-karam) — Generosity
The highest virtue. Jordanians will go broke to host you well.

### العيب (il-3eib) — Shame
Social pressure to behave correctly. "3eib" is said when someone does something socially unacceptable.

### الوجه (il-wajh) — Face/Honor
Saving face is critical. Don't embarrass anyone publicly.

### الواسطة (il-waas6a) — Connections
Using personal connections to get things done. It's how Jordan operates.

### القعدة (il-2a3de) — The Gathering
Sitting together for tea/coffee is sacred social time. Don't rush it.
