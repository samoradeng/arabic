# Listening & Speaking Guide — How to Actually Get Fluent

Reading Arabic is step 1. Hearing it and speaking it is where fluency lives. This guide tells you exactly what to do.

---

## The 3-Week Audio Schedule

### Week 1: Build Your Ear (Days 1-7)
**Goal:** Recognize individual words when spoken

| Daily Activity | Time | What to Do |
|----------------|------|------------|
| Flashcards with audio | 20 min | Use practice.html — tap speaker on EVERY card, repeat out loud |
| Listening Quiz | 15 min | practice.html "Listening Quiz" — hear Arabic, pick meaning |
| Shadowing | 15 min | practice.html "Shadowing" — listen 3x, repeat 3x |
| Conversation audio | 10 min | Play full conversations, follow along, repeat each line |
| YouTube immersion | 30 min | Search "Jordanian Arabic conversation" — just listen, don't stress about understanding everything |

### Week 2: Start Catching Phrases (Days 8-14)
**Goal:** Understand sentences at moderate speed

| Daily Activity | Time | What to Do |
|----------------|------|------------|
| Shadowing (harder) | 20 min | Use "Fast" speed. Shadow full dialogue lines, not just words |
| Listening Quiz | 15 min | Try to answer BEFORE the choices appear (cover screen) |
| YouTube with pausing | 30 min | Watch Jordanian content, pause every 30 seconds, write what you heard |
| Speak to yourself | 15 min | Narrate what you're doing in Arabic: "هسا بروح عالمطبخ. بدي آكل." |
| Record yourself | 10 min | Use your phone. Record yourself saying 10 phrases. Listen back. |

### Week 3: Real Conversation (Days 15-21)
**Goal:** Understand native speed, respond naturally

| Daily Activity | Time | What to Do |
|----------------|------|------------|
| Conversation partner | 30 min | Find someone on italki, Tandem, or HelloTalk |
| Full dialogue shadowing | 20 min | Shadow entire conversations at normal speed |
| YouTube without subtitles | 20 min | Watch Jordanian vlogs. Write summary of what you understood. |
| Think in Arabic | All day | Label everything in Arabic. Internal monologue in Arabic. |
| Teach someone | 15 min | Explain a grammar point to a friend (in Arabic if possible) |

## The Shadowing Method (Most Important Technique)

Shadowing = listening to Arabic and repeating it **simultaneously or immediately after**, matching:
- The **sounds** (pronunciation)
- The **rhythm** (which syllables are stressed)
- The **speed** (as close to native as possible)
- The **intonation** (how the voice goes up and down)

### How to Shadow:

1. **Listen first** — hear the full phrase once without repeating
2. **Slow shadow** — play at slow speed, repeat after each phrase
3. **Normal shadow** — play at normal speed, try to keep up
4. **Mirror shadow** — speak at the SAME TIME as the audio
5. **Free recall** — turn off audio, say it from memory

**Do 10-15 minutes of shadowing EVERY DAY.** This single technique will improve your pronunciation and listening more than anything else.

### What to Listen For:
- The بـ prefix on present tense verbs
- The glottal stop (hamza) replacing ق
- Phrases like يلا، طيب، يعني، والله
- The rhythm and melody of Jordanian speech

## Speaking Tips

### 1. Don't Translate in Your Head
Stop thinking "How do I say 'I want water' in Arabic?" Instead, see water → بدي ميّ should be automatic.

### 2. Mumble Practice
When you're alone, mumble Arabic phrases under your breath. Walking, cooking, showering — just keep Arabic sounds coming out of your mouth.

### 3. The 5-Second Rule
When someone speaks Arabic to you, try to respond within 5 seconds. Don't aim for perfection — aim for speed. Wrong grammar is fine; silence kills fluency.

### 4. Record & Compare
1. Listen to a native say a phrase (practice.html audio)
2. Record yourself saying the same phrase
3. Listen to both back to back
4. Identify the difference
5. Try again

### 5. Talk to Yourself
Narrate your day in Arabic:
- "هسا بقوم من النوم. بروح عالحمام. بتحمم. بلبس. بفطر."
- "بدي أروح على السوبرماركت. بدي أشتري خبز وحليب."

This trains your brain to think in Arabic without a conversation partner.

## Common Pronunciation Mistakes

| Mistake | Correct | Tip |
|---------|---------|-----|
| Saying ق as "q" | Say ء (glottal stop) | Jordanians say 2aal not qaal |
| Flat ع (ain) | Deep throat squeeze | Practice saying "aaaa" from deep in your throat |
| Weak ح (7a) | Strong breathy H | Like fogging a mirror, but harder |
| Rolling ر | Tap with tongue tip | Similar to Spanish "r" |
| Ignoring emphasis on ط ص ض ظ | Heavier, deeper versions | These sound "thicker" than their non-emphatic versions |

## Daily Minimum (Non-Negotiable)

If you do NOTHING else, do these 3 things every day:

1. **10 min shadowing** — practice.html Shadowing mode
2. **5 min listening quiz** — practice.html Listening Quiz
3. **5 min self-narration** — describe what you're doing in Arabic

That's 20 minutes. No excuses. Do this and you WILL hear improvement by Week 2.
