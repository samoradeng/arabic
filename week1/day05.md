## 2. Restaurant Phrases — بالمطعم

### Entering & Seating

| Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning |
|--------|----------------|---------|
| في طاولة فاضية؟ | fii 6aawle faa9ye? | Is there an empty table? |
| لشخصين | la-shakhs-ein | For two people |
| المنيو، لو سمحت | il-menyu, law sama7t | The menu, please |
| شو عندكم اليوم؟ | shu 3indkum il-yoom? | What do you have today? |
| شو بتنصحني؟ | shu bitinsa7ni? | What do you recommend? |

### Ordering

| Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning |
|--------|----------------|---------|
| بدي... | biddi... | I want... |
| ممكن...؟ | mumkin...? | Can I have...? |
| بدي أطلب | biddi a6lub | I'd like to order |
| أعطيني... | a36eeni... | Give me... |
| واحد منسف، لو سمحت | waa7ad mansaf, law sama7t | One mansaf, please |
| بدون بصل | bidoon ba9al | without onion |
| مع خبز إضافي | ma3 khubz i9aafi | with extra bread |
| حار ولا مش حار؟ | 7aarr willa mish 7aarr? | Spicy or not spicy? |
| مش حار، لو سمحت | mish 7aarr, law sama7t | Not spicy, please |

### During the Meal

| Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning |
|--------|----------------|---------|

| Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning |
|--------|----------------|---------|

## 3. The Request Forms — بدي / ممكن

### بدي (biddi) — I want

This is the most common way to express desire in Jordanian Arabic:

| Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning |
|--------|----------------|---------|
| بدي | biddi | I want |
| بدك | biddak (m) / biddik (f) | you want |
| بده | biddu | he wants |
| بدها | bidha | she wants |
| بدنا | bidna | we want |
| بدكم | bidkum | you (pl) want |
| بدهم | bidhum | they want |

**Examples:**
- بدي آكل — biddi aakul — I want to eat
- بدي أروح — biddi aroo7 — I want to go
- شو بدك؟ — shu biddak? — What do you want?

### ممكن (mumkin) — More polite

- ممكن ميّ؟ — mumkin mayy? — Can I have water?
- ممكن الحساب؟ — mumkin il-7saab? — Can I have the bill?

### لو سمحت (law sama7t) — Please / Excuse me

Add this to anything to be polite:
- لو سمحت، وين الحمام؟ — law sama7t, wein il-7ammaam? — Excuse me, where's the bathroom?

## 4. Full Restaurant Dialogue

> — أهلا وسهلا! تفضلو.
> — ahla w sahla! tfa99alu.
> — *Welcome! Come in / sit down.*

> — شكرا. في طاولة لاثنين؟
> — shukran. fii 6aawle la-ithnein?
> — *Thanks. Is there a table for two?*

> — تفضلو هون. هاي المنيو.
> — tfa99alu hoon. haay il-menyu.
> — *Sit here. Here's the menu.*

> — شكرا. شو بتنصحنا؟
> — shukran. shu bitinsa7na?
> — *Thanks. What do you recommend?*

> — المنسف زاكي كتير اليوم. والمقلوبة كمان.
> — il-mansaf zaaki kteer il-yoom. wil-ma2loobe kamaan.
> — *The mansaf is very good today. And the maqlube too.*

> — طيب. بدي منسف. وصاحبي بده مقلوبة.
> — 6ayyib. biddi mansaf. w 9aa7bi biddu ma2loobe.
> — *OK. I want mansaf. And my friend wants maqlube.*

> — تمام. تشربو شي؟
> — tamaam. tishrabo shi?
> — *OK. Do you want to drink anything?*

> — آه، اثنين شاي بالميرمية، لو سمحت.
> — aah, ithnein shaay bil-meiramiyye, law sama7t.
> — *Yes, two sage teas, please.*

> *(after eating)*

> — صحتين!
> — 9a7tein!
> — *Bon appétit!*

> — الله يصحك. الأكل زاكي كتير! تسلم إيديك.
> — allah yi97ak. il-akil zaaki kteer! tislam ideik.
> — *Thank you. The food is very delicious! Bless your hands.*

> — الحساب، لو سمحت.
> — il-7saab, law sama7t.
> — *The bill, please.*

## 5. Exercises

### Exercise A: Order these items in Jordanian Arabic
1. One shawarma, please
2. I want Arabic coffee without sugar
3. Two falafel sandwiches and one lemonade
4. The mansaf, is it spicy?

### Exercise B: Respond in Arabic
1. Waiter says: صحتين — you say?
2. Someone asks: شو بدك تشرب؟ — you want tea
3. Waiter asks: كاش ولا فيزا؟ — you want cash
4. You want to compliment the cook

### Exercise C: Role-play the full restaurant scenario
Read the dialogue above, then try to write your own restaurant interaction from memory — entering, ordering, paying. Write at least 8 lines of dialogue.
