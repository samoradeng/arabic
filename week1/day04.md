## 1. Present Tense in Jordanian Arabic

Jordanian Arabic uses a **بـ (b-)** prefix on present tense verbs to indicate habitual/ongoing actions. This is different from MSA.

### The Pattern

The base form comes from the MSA present (مضارع), but with the بـ prefix:

| Pronoun | Prefix | Example: يروح (to go) | Transliteration |
|---------|--------|-----------------------|----------------|
| أنا | بـ | بروح | baroo7 |
| إنتَ | بتـ | بتروح | bitroo7 |
| إنتِ | بتـ | بتروحي | bitroo7i |
| هو | بـ | بروح | biroo7 |
| هي | بتـ | بتروح | bitroo7 |
| إحنا | منـ / بنـ | بنروح | binroo7 |
| إنتو | بتـ | بتروحو | bitroo7u |
| هم | بـ | بروحو | biroo7u |

> **Key Insight:** The بـ prefix is what makes Jordanian/Levantine Arabic sound different from MSA and Egyptian. Without it, the verb is subjunctive (used after certain particles).

## 3. Negation — مش / ما + بـ

To negate present tense verbs, use **ما** before the verb:

| Positive | Negative | Meaning |
|----------|----------|---------|
| بروح | ما بروح | I don't go |
| بفهم | ما بفهم | I don't understand |
| بعرف | ما بعرف | I don't know |
| بحب | ما بحب | I don't like |

**ما بعرف** (maa ba3rif — "I don't know") is probably the phrase you'll use most in your first week.

## 4. Daily Routine — الروتين اليومي

### Routine Vocabulary

| Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning |
|--------|----------------|---------|
| بقوم من النوم | ba2oom min in-noom | I wake up |
| بتحمم | bat7ammam | I shower |
| بلبس | balbas | I get dressed |
| بفطر | baf6ur | I eat breakfast |
| بطلع من البيت | ba6la3 min il-beit | I leave the house |
| بروح عالشغل | baroo7 3ash-shughul | I go to work |
| بتغدى | batghadda | I eat lunch |
| برجع عالبيت | barja3 3al-beit | I go home |
| بتعشى | bat3asha | I eat dinner |
| بتفرج عالتلفزيون | batfarraj 3at-tilfizyoon | I watch TV |
| بنام | banaam | I sleep |

### Daily Routine Dialogue

> — شو بتعمل كل يوم؟
> — shu bta3mal kull yoom?
> — *What do you do every day?*

> — بقوم الساعة ستة الصبح. بتحمم وبلبس وبفطر.
> — ba2oom is-saa3a sitte i9-9ub7. bat7ammam w balbas w baf6ur.
> — *I get up at 6 in the morning. I shower, get dressed, and eat breakfast.*

> — وبعدين؟
> — w ba3dein?
> — *And then?*

> — بطلع من البيت الساعة سبعة و نص. بروح عالشغل بالسيارة.
> — ba6la3 min il-beit is-saa3a sab3a w nu99. baroo7 3ash-shughul bis-sayyaara.
> — *I leave the house at 7:30. I go to work by car.*

> — إمتى برجع؟
> — imta birja3?
> — *When do you come back?*

> — بعد الساعة خمسة. بتغدى وبرتاح شوي. بالليل بتعلم عربي!
> — ba3d is-saa3a khamse. batghadda w bartaa7 shwayy. bil-leil bat3allam 3arabi!
> — *After 5. I eat lunch and rest a bit. At night I learn Arabic!*

## 5. Question Words — كلمات الاستفهام

| Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning |
|--------|----------------|---------|
| شو؟ | shu? | What? |
| مين؟ | meen? | Who? |
| وين؟ | wein? | Where? |
| إمتى؟ | imta? | When? |
| ليش؟ | leish? | Why? |
| كيف؟ | keef? | How? |
| قديش؟ | 2addeish? | How much/many? |
| أي؟ | ayy? | Which? |

### Making Questions

Simple — just use question word + verb:

- **شو بتشتغل؟** — shu bitishtaghil? — What do you do for work?
- **وين بتسكن؟** — wein btiskun? — Where do you live?
- **ليش ما بتروح؟** — leish maa bitroo7? — Why don't you go?
- **كيف بتروح عالشغل؟** — keef bitroo7 3ash-shughul? — How do you go to work?
- **قديش بتدفع؟** — 2addeish bitidfa3? — How much do you pay?
