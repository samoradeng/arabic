# Day 7: Week 1 Review & Test

## Review Checklist

Before taking the test, make sure you can:

- [ ] Greet someone and introduce yourself fluently
- [ ] Describe your family
- [ ] Count to 100 and tell time
- [ ] Conjugate present tense verbs with بـ prefix
- [ ] Order food at a restaurant
- [ ] Ask for and understand basic directions
- [ ] Use question words (شو، وين، إمتى، ليش، كيف، قديش)
- [ ] Negate verbs with ما
- [ ] Use بدي (I want) + verb
- [ ] Use possessive suffixes

## Week 1 Comprehensive Test

### Part 1: Translation — English to Jordanian Arabic (20 points)

Translate these sentences:

1. Hello, how are you? (to a woman)
2. My name is [your name]. I'm from [your country]. Pleased to meet you.
3. I have two brothers and one sister.
4. I wake up at 7 in the morning every day.
5. I want shawarma without onion, please.
6. Where is the nearest pharmacy?
7. Go straight then turn right after the traffic light.
8. How much is this? (at a market)
9. I don't understand. I speak a little Arabic.
10. The food is very delicious. Bless your hands.

### Part 2: Translation — Jordanian Arabic to English (20 points)

1. أهلا وسهلا فيك! من وين إنت؟
2. أختي بتدرس بالجامعة. هي شاطرة كتير.
3. الساعة ثلاثة و نص. المحل بسكر الساعة عشرة بالليل.
4. ما بعرف وين المطعم. ممكن تسأل هداك الزلمة.
5. بدي أروح على وسط البلد بالتاكسي. قديش بدك؟
6. بقوم الصبح، بتحمم وبفطر، وبعدين بروح عالشغل.
7. بكرة الجمعة. ما في شغل.
8. شو بتنصحني؟ بدي آكل أكل أردني.
9. الحساب لو سمحت. كاش.
10. لف شمال بعد الدوار. المستشفى على يمينك.

### Part 3: Conversation (20 points)

Write a complete dialogue (at least 12 lines) where you:
1. Meet someone new
2. Introduce yourself
3. Talk about your family
4. Ask what they do for work
5. Say goodbye

### Part 4: Situation Responses (20 points)

What would you say in Jordanian Arabic in these situations?

1. You enter a shop where the owner is working.
2. Someone compliments your Arabic.
3. You want to know the time.
4. You're full and don't want more food.
5. You're lost and need to find a hotel.
6. The taxi driver charges too much.
7. You want to order Arabic coffee with no sugar.
8. Someone says مع السلامة to you.
9. You want to sit down at a restaurant.
10. Your friend introduces you to their brother.

### Part 5: Verb Conjugation (20 points)

Conjugate these verbs in present tense for all 8 pronouns:

1. بروح (to go)
2. باكل (to eat)

## Answer Key

### Part 1:
1. مرحبا، كيفك؟
2. اسمي ___. أنا من ___. تشرفنا.
3. عندي أخين (or اثنين إخوان) وأخت وحدة.
4. بقوم الساعة سبعة الصبح كل يوم.
5. بدي شاورما بدون بصل، لو سمحت.
6. وين أقرب صيدلية؟
7. روح دغري وبعدين لف يمين بعد الإشارة.
8. قديش هاد؟ / بقديش هاد؟
9. ما بفهم. بحكي شوي عربي.
10. الأكل زاكي كتير. تسلم إيديك.

### Part 2:
1. Welcome! Where are you from?
2. My sister studies at university. She's very clever.
3. It's 3:30. The shop closes at 10 at night.
4. I don't know where the restaurant is. You can ask that man.
5. I want to go to downtown by taxi. How much do you want?
6. I wake up in the morning, shower, eat breakfast, then go to work.
7. Tomorrow is Friday. No work.
8. What do you recommend? I want to eat Jordanian food.
9. The bill please. Cash.
10. Turn left after the roundabout. The hospital is on your right.

### Part 4:
1. يعطيك العافية — ya36eek il-3aafye
2. الله يخليك / شكرا — allah ykhalleek / shukran
3. قديش الساعة؟ — 2addeish is-saa3a?
4. بس، شبعت. شكرا. — bass, shba3t. shukran.
5. لو سمحت، وين أقرب فندق؟ — law sama7t, wein a2rab fundu2?
6. كتير! غالي! — kteer! ghaali!
7. بدي قهوة عربية بدون سكر — biddi 2ahwe 3arabiyye bidoon sukkar
8. الله معك — allah ma3ak
9. في طاولة فاضية؟ — fii 6aawle faa9ye?
10. تشرفنا — tsharrafna
