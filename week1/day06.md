# Day 6: Directions & Transportation

## Lesson Goals
- Ask for and understand directions
- Use transportation (taxis, buses)
- Navigate a city in Arabic
- Learn prepositions of place

## 3. Transportation — المواصلات

### Types of Transport

| Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning |
|--------|----------------|---------|
| سيارة | sayyaara | car |
| تاكسي | taksi | taxi |
| باص | baa9 | bus |
| سرفيس | sarfees | shared minibus (very common in Jordan) |
| طيارة | 6ayyaara | airplane |

### Taxi Phrases

| Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning |
|--------|----------------|---------|
| تاكسي! | taksi! | Taxi! |
| على وسط البلد | 3ala wasa6 il-balad | To downtown |
| على المطار | 3al-ma6aar | To the airport |
| قديش عالعداد؟ | 2addeish 3al-3addaad? | How much on the meter? |
| شغل العداد | shaghghil il-3addaad | Turn on the meter |
| قديش بدك؟ | 2addeish biddak? | How much do you want? (negotiate) |
| كتير! غالي | kteer! ghaali | Too much! Expensive |
| وقف هون | wa22if hoon | Stop here |
| على هالناصية | 3a hal-naa9ye | At this corner |
| خليها | khaleeha | Keep the change |

> **Cultural Note:** In Amman, some taxis use meters, many don't. Agree on a price before getting in, or insist on the meter (شغل العداد). It's normal to negotiate.

### Bus Phrases

| Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning |
|--------|----------------|---------|
| هاد الباص بروح على...؟ | haad il-baa9 biroo7 3ala...? | Does this bus go to...? |
| وين بنزل لـ...؟ | wein binzil la...? | Where do I get off for...? |
| بدي أنزل! | biddi anzil! | I want to get off! |
| قديش التذكرة؟ | 2addeish it-tadhkara? | How much is the ticket? |

## 4. Direction Dialogue

> — لو سمحت، وين المول؟
> — law sama7t, wein il-mool?
> — *Excuse me, where is the mall?*

> — المول؟ مش بعيد. روح دغري على هالشارع...
> — il-mool? mish b3eed. roo7 dughri 3ala hash-shaari3...
> — *The mall? Not far. Go straight on this street...*

> — آه...
> — aah...
> — *Yes...*

> — بعد الإشارة الثانية، لف يمين. بتشوف الدوار. المول بعد الدوار على الشمال.
> — ba3d il-ishaare ith-thaanye, liff yameen. bitshoof id-dawwaar. il-mool ba3d id-dawwaar 3ash-shmaal.
> — *After the second traffic light, turn right. You'll see the roundabout. The mall is after the roundabout on the left.*

> — تقريبا قديش بعيد؟
> — ta2reeban 2addeish b3eed?
> — *Roughly how far?*

> — عشر دقايق مشي.
> — 3ashar da2aayi2 mashi.
> — *Ten minutes walking.*

> — شكرا كتير!
> — shukran kteer!
> — *Thank you so much!*

> — ولا يهمك!
> — wala yhimmak!
> — *Don't mention it!*

## 5. Taxi Dialogue

> — تاكسي! على الدوار السابع، لو سمحت.
> — taksi! 3ad-dawwaar is-saabi3, law sama7t.
> — *Taxi! To the 7th circle, please.*

> — يلا اطلع. قديش بتدفع؟
> — yalla i6la3. 2addeish bitidfa3?
> — *Get in. How much will you pay?*

> — شغل العداد.
> — shaghghil il-3addaad.
> — *Turn on the meter.*

> — ما في عداد. ثلاث دنانير.
> — maa fii 3addaad. thalath danaaneer.
> — *No meter. Three dinars.*

> — كتير! دينارين.
> — kteer! dinaarein.
> — *Too much! Two dinars.*

> — طيب، يلا.
> — 6ayyib, yalla.
> — *OK, let's go.*

> *(arriving)*

> — وقف هون، لو سمحت.
> — wa22if hoon, law sama7t.
> — *Stop here, please.*

> — تفضل.
> — tfa99al.
> — *Here you go.*

> — شكرا. خليها.
> — shukran. khaleeha.
> — *Thanks. Keep the change.*
