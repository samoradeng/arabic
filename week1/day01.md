## 1. Greetings — التحيات

Jordanians greet *a lot*. Greetings are layered — you often exchange 3-4 before getting to the point.

### Essential Greetings

| Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning | Response |
|--------|----------------|---------|----------|
| مرحبا | mar7aba | Hello | مرحبتين — mar7abtein (double hello) |
| أهلا وسهلا | ahla w sahla | Welcome | أهلا فيك — ahla feek (m) / feeki (f) |
| السلام عليكم | is-salaamu 3aleikum | Peace be upon you | وعليكم السلام — wa 3aleikum is-salaam |
| كيفك؟ | keefak (m) / keefik (f) | How are you? | الحمد لله، تمام — il-7amdu lillah, tamaam |
| شو أخبارك؟ | shu akhbaarak (m) / akhbaarik (f) | What's your news? | كلشي تمام — kulshi tamaam (everything's good) |
| صباح الخير | 9abaa7 il-kheir | Good morning | صباح النور — 9abaa7 in-noor |
| مساء الخير | masa2 il-kheir | Good evening | مساء النور — masa2 in-noor |
| يعطيك العافية | ya36eek il-3aafye | May God give you strength | الله يعافيك — allah y3aafeek |

### Goodbye Phrases

| Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning |
|--------|----------------|---------|
| مع السلامة | ma3 is-salaame | Goodbye (with safety) |
| الله معك | allah ma3ak (m) / ma3ik (f) | God be with you |
| يلا باي | yalla bye | Alright, bye (casual) |
| بشوفك | bshoofak (m) / bshoofik (f) | I'll see you |

> **Cultural Note:** يعطيك العافية (ya36eek il-3aafye) is used *constantly* — when someone is working, when you enter a shop, when you see anyone doing anything. It's the Swiss Army knife of Jordanian politeness.

## 2. Personal Pronouns — الضمائر

Jordanian Arabic simplifies MSA pronouns. There's no dual form in casual speech.

| English | Arabic | Transliteration |
|---------|--------|----------------|
| I | أنا | ana |
| You (m) | إنتَ | inte |
| You (f) | إنتِ | inti |
| He | هو | huwwe |
| She | هي | hiyye |
| We | إحنا | i7na |
| You (plural) | إنتو | intu |
| They | هم | humme |

> **Key Jordanian feature:** Notice هو is pronounced *huwwe* (not just *huwa* like MSA) and هي is *hiyye* (not *hiya*). This is a dead giveaway of Levantine dialect.

## 3. Introductions — التعارف

### Key Phrases

| Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning |
|--------|----------------|---------|
| شو اسمك؟ | shu ismak (m) / ismik (f) | What's your name? |
| اسمي... | ismi... | My name is... |
| تشرفنا | tsharrafna | Pleased to meet you |
| من وين إنت؟ | min wein inte (m) / inti (f) | Where are you from? |
| أنا من... | ana min... | I'm from... |
| شو بتشتغل؟ | shu bitishtaghil (m) / bitishtaghli (f) | What do you do (for work)? |
| أنا بشتغل... | ana bashtaghil... | I work as... |
| أنا طالب/طالبة | ana 6aalib / 6aalibe | I'm a student (m/f) |
| عمري... سنة | 3umri... sane | I'm ... years old |
| بحكي شوي عربي | ba7ki shwayy 3arabi | I speak a little Arabic |
| بتعلم عربي | bat3allam 3arabi | I'm learning Arabic |

### Introduction Dialogue

**Practice reading this aloud 5 times:**

> — مرحبا! كيفك؟
> — mar7aba! keefak?
> — *Hello! How are you?*

> — الحمد لله، تمام. وإنت؟
> — il-7amdu lillah, tamaam. w inte?
> — *Thank God, good. And you?*

> — تمام، الحمد لله. شو اسمك؟
> — tamaam, il-7amdu lillah. shu ismak?
> — *Good, thank God. What's your name?*

> — اسمي أحمد. وإنت؟
> — ismi A7mad. w inte?
> — *My name is Ahmad. And you?*

> — اسمي مارك. تشرفنا.
> — ismi Mark. tsharrafna.
> — *My name is Mark. Pleased to meet you.*

> — أهلا وسهلا. من وين إنت؟
> — ahla w sahla. min wein inte?
> — *Welcome. Where are you from?*

> — أنا من أمريكا. بتعلم عربي.
> — ana min amreeka. bat3allam 3arabi.
> — *I'm from America. I'm learning Arabic.*

> — والله؟ أهلا وسهلا فيك! عربيك منيح!
> — wallah? ahla w sahla feek! 3arabiyyak mnee7!
> — *Really? Welcome! Your Arabic is good!*

## 4. Essential Response Words

These tiny words are the glue of Jordanian conversation:

| Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning | Usage |
|--------|----------------|---------|-------|
| آه | aah | Yes | Casual yes |
| لا | la2 | No | |
| طيب | 6ayyib | OK / Good / Alright | Used constantly |
| يلا | yalla | Let's go / Come on | Most versatile word |
| إن شاء الله | inshaallah | God willing | Future plans, or polite "maybe" |
| ما شاء الله | mashaallah | God has willed it | Compliment, admiration |
| والله | wallah | I swear / Really? | Emphasis or surprise |
| يعني | ya3ni | It means / Like | Filler word, like English "like" |
| هيك | heik | Like this / So | "That's how it is" |
