## Lesson Goals
- Count from 1-100 in Jordanian Arabic

### Combining: ones + و + tens
- 21 = واحد وعشرين — waa7ad w 3ishreen
- 35 = خمسة وثلاثين — khamse w thalaatheen
- 99 = تسعة وتسعين — tis3a w tis3een

> **Tip:** In Jordanian Arabic, people often just say the last two digits for prices. "25" might just be "khamse w 3ishreen" without specifying currency.

## 2. Days of the Week — أيام الأسبوع

| Arabic | Transliteration | English |
|--------|----------------|---------|
| الأحد | il-a7ad | Sunday |
| الاثنين | il-ithnein | Monday |
| الثلاثاء | it-thalaatha2 | Tuesday |
| الأربعاء | il-arba3a2 | Wednesday |
| الخميس | il-khamees | Thursday |
| الجمعة | ij-jum3a | Friday |
| السبت | is-sabit | Saturday |

> **Cultural Note:** The weekend in Jordan is **Friday-Saturday**. Friday is the holy day (like Sunday in Christian countries). Thursday evening is when people go out — it's their "Friday night."

## 3. Telling Time — الساعة

### Asking the time

**كم الساعة؟** — kam is-saa3a? — What time is it?

### Hours

| Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning |
|--------|----------------|---------|
| الساعة وحدة | is-saa3a wa7de | It's 1:00 |
| الساعة ثنتين | is-saa3a thintein | It's 2:00 |
| الساعة ثلاثة | is-saa3a thalathe | It's 3:00 |
| الساعة اثنعش | is-saa3a ithna3sh | It's 12:00 |

### Minutes and Fractions

| Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning |
|--------|----------------|---------|
| و ربع | w rub3 | quarter past |
| و نص | w nu99 | half past |
| إلا ربع | illa rub3 | quarter to |
| و عشر دقايق | w 3ashar da2aayi2 | and 10 minutes |

**Examples:**
- الساعة ثلاثة و نص — is-saa3a thalathe w nu99 — 3:30
- الساعة سبعة إلا ربع — is-saa3a sab3a illa rub3 — 6:45
- الساعة وحدة و ربع — is-saa3a wa7de w rub3 — 1:15

### Time of Day

| Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning |
|--------|----------------|---------|
| الصبح | i9-9ub7 | morning |
| الظهر | i6'-6uhur | noon |
| بعد الظهر | ba3d i6'-6uhur | afternoon |
| المغرب | il-maghrib | sunset |
| الليل | il-leil | night |

## 4. Months — الأشهر

Jordan uses **Levantine month names** (not the Gulf/Egyptian ones):

| Arabic | Transliteration | English |
|--------|----------------|---------|
| كانون الثاني | kaanoon ith-thaani | January |
| شباط | shbaa6 | February |
| آذار | adhaar | March |
| نيسان | neesaan | April |
| أيار | ayyaar | May |
| حزيران | 7uzeiraan | June |
| تموز | tammooz | July |
| آب | aab | August |
| أيلول | eilool | September |
| تشرين الأول | tishreem il-awwal | October |
| تشرين الثاني | tishreem ith-thaani | November |
| كانون الأول | kaanoon il-awwal | December |

> **Note:** These Levantine month names come from ancient Babylonian/Aramaic names. They're used in Jordan, Syria, Palestine, Lebanon, and Iraq.

## 5. Time Dialogue

> — قديش الساعة هلأ؟
> — 2addeish is-saa3a halla2?
> — *What time is it now?*

> — الساعة أربعة و نص.
> — is-saa3a arba3a w nu99.
> — *It's 4:30.*

> — طيب، إمتى بتفتح المحلات؟
> — 6ayyib, imta bitifta7 il-ma7allaat?
> — *OK, when do the shops open?*

> — الصبح الساعة عشرة. بس اليوم جمعة، يمكن بعد الظهر.
> — i9-9ub7 is-saa3a 3ashara. bass il-yoom jum3a, yimkin ba3d i6'-6uhur.
> — *In the morning at 10. But today is Friday, maybe afternoon.*

> — ماشي. وإمتى بتسكر؟
> — maashi. w imta bitsakkar?
> — *OK. And when do they close?*

> — الساعة عشرة بالليل.
> — is-saa3a 3ashara bil-leil.
> — *10 at night.*
