# Day 8: Past Tense & Storytelling

## Lesson Goals
- Conjugate past tense verbs in Jordanian Arabic
- Tell stories about what happened
- Use time expressions for the past
- Connect sentences with conjunctions

---

## 1. Past Tense — الماضي

Past tense in Arabic uses **suffixes** (no بـ prefix). The base form is the "he" form.

### Conjugation Pattern

Using راح (raa7 — went):

| Pronoun | Arabic | Transliteration |
|---------|--------|----------------|
| أنا | رحت | ru7t |
| إنتَ | رحت | ru7t |
| إنتِ | رحتي | ru7ti |
| هو | راح | raa7 |
| هي | راحت | raa7at |
| إحنا | رحنا | ru7na |
| إنتو | رحتو | ru7tu |
| هم | راحو | raa7u |

### Pattern Summary (Suffixes)

| Pronoun | Suffix | Example with أكل (akal — ate) |
|---------|--------|-------------------------------|
| أنا | -t | أكلت — akalt |
| إنتَ | -t | أكلت — akalt |
| إنتِ | -ti | أكلتي — akalti |
| هو | — (base) | أكل — akal |
| هي | -at | أكلت — aklat |
| إحنا | -na | أكلنا — akalna |
| إنتو | -tu | أكلتو — akaltu |
| هم | -u | أكلو — akalu |

> **Note:** أنا and إنتَ have the same past tense form. Context makes it clear.

## 3. Negating Past Tense — ما + verb

Simply add **ما** before the past tense verb:

| Positive | Negative | Meaning |
|----------|----------|---------|
| رحت | ما رحت | I didn't go |
| أكلنا | ما أكلنا | We didn't eat |
| شاف | ما شاف | He didn't see |
| فهمت | ما فهمت | I didn't understand |

## 6. Storytelling Dialogue — "What did you do yesterday?"

> — شو سويت إمبارح؟
> — shu sawweit imbaarih?
> — *What did you do yesterday?*

> — إمبارح كان يوم طويل! قمت الصبح الساعة ستة.
> — imbaarih kaan yoom 6aweel! 2umt i9-9ub7 is-saa3a sitte.
> — *Yesterday was a long day! I got up in the morning at 6.*

> — بدري هيك! ليش؟
> — badri heik! leish?
> — *That early! Why?*

> — لأنه كان عندي اجتماع بالشغل الساعة ثمانية. رحت بالتاكسي.
> — la2innu kaan 3indi ijtimaa3 bish-shughul is-saa3a thamaanye. ru7t bit-taksi.
> — *Because I had a meeting at work at 8. I went by taxi.*

> — وبعدين؟
> — w ba3dein?
> — *And then?*

> — بعد الاجتماع، اشتغلت لحد الساعة وحدة. بعدين تغديت مع صحابي بمطعم.
> — ba3d il-ijtimaa3, ishtazhalt la-7add is-saa3a wa7de. ba3dein tghaddeit ma3 s7aabi b-ma63am.
> — *After the meeting, I worked until 1. Then I had lunch with my friends at a restaurant.*

> — شو أكلتو؟
> — shu akaltu?
> — *What did you eat?*

> — منسف! كان زاكي كتير. بعد الغدا رجعت عالبيت وارتحت شوي.
> — mansaf! kaan zaaki kteer. ba3d il-ghada raja3t 3al-beit w irta7t shwayy.
> — *Mansaf! It was very delicious. After lunch I went home and rested a bit.*

> — حلو. وبالليل؟
> — 7ilu. w bil-leil?
> — *Nice. And at night?*

> — بالليل درست عربي ساعتين! وبعدين نمت.
> — bil-leil darast 3arabi saa3tein! w ba3dein nimt.
> — *At night I studied Arabic for two hours! And then I slept.*

## 7. كان (kaan) — Was / Were

كان is the past tense of "to be" — which doesn't exist in present tense (Day 2 lesson).

| Pronoun | Arabic | Transliteration |
|---------|--------|----------------|
| أنا | كنت | kunt |
| إنتَ | كنت | kunt |
| إنتِ | كنتي | kunti |
| هو | كان | kaan |
| هي | كانت | kaanat |
| إحنا | كنا | kunna |
| إنتو | كنتو | kuntu |
| هم | كانو | kaanu |

**Examples:**
- كنت تعبان — kunt ta3baan — I was tired
- كانت الحفلة حلوة — kaanat il-7afle 7ilwe — The party was nice
- كنا بالبيت — kunna bil-beit — We were at home
- ما كان عنده فلوس — maa kaan 3indu floos — He didn't have money
