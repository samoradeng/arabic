## 3. Bargaining — المفاوضة

Bargaining is expected at souks and small shops. NOT at malls or supermarkets.

### The Bargaining Script

1. **Ask the price:** قديش هاد؟ — 2addeish haad?
2. **React with shock:** كتير! / غالي! — kteer! / ghaali!
3. **Counter-offer at 50-60%:** أعطيك ___. — a36eek ___.
4. **They counter:** لا، آخر سعر ___. — la2, aakhir si3r ___.
5. **Walk away technique:** طيب، يلا، شكرا. — 6ayyib, yalla, shukran.
6. **They call you back** or you accept: طيب، ماشي — 6ayyib, maashi

### Bargaining Dialogue

> — قديش هاد الكندرة؟
> — 2addeish haad il-kundara?
> — *How much are these shoes?*

> — هاي ثلاثين دينار. نوعية ممتازة.
> — haay thalaatheen deenaar. noo3iyye mumtaaze.
> — *These are 30 dinars. Excellent quality.*

> — ثلاثين؟! كتير يا عمي! أعطيك خمسطعش.
> — thalaatheen?! kteer ya 3ammi! a36eek khamas6a3sh.
> — *Thirty?! Too much, uncle! I'll give you 15.*

> — خمسطعش؟! مش معقول! هاي جلد أصلي. خمسة وعشرين.
> — khamas6a3sh?! mish ma32ool! haay jild a9li. khamse w 3ishreen.
> — *15?! No way! These are genuine leather. 25.*

> — عشرين وبأخدها.
> — 3ishreen w baakhidha.
> — *20 and I'll take them.*

> — طيب، بس لأنك ابن حلال. عشرين.
> — 6ayyib, bass la2innak ibin 7alaal. 3ishreen.
> — *OK, but only because you're a good person. 20.*

> **Key phrase:** يا عمي (ya 3ammi — "uncle") is used with shop owners to be friendly and warm. It's not literal.

## 4. Comparisons — المقارنة

### Forming Comparisons

Pattern: **أَفْعَل** (af3al) — similar to English "-er"

| Adjective | Comparative | Meaning |
|-----------|-------------|---------|
| كبير (big) | أكبر (akbar) | bigger |
| صغير (small) | أصغر (azghar) | smaller |
| غالي (expensive) | أغلى (aghla) | more expensive |
| رخيص (cheap) | أرخص (arkha9) | cheaper |
| حلو (nice) | أحلى (a7la) | nicer |
| منيح (good) | أحسن (a7san) | better |
| كتير (much) | أكتر (aktar) | more |
| قريب (near) | أقرب (a2rab) | nearer |
| بعيد (far) | أبعد (ab3ad) | farther |

### Using Comparisons

**X أفعل من Y** — X is ___er than Y

- هاد أرخص من هداك — haad arkha9 min hadaak — This is cheaper than that
- أختي أكبر مني — ukhti akbar minni — My sister is older than me
- الأردن أحلى من... — il-urdun a7la min... — Jordan is nicer than...

## 5. Exercises

### Exercise A: Bargaining role-play
Write a bargaining dialogue for buying a jacket. Start at 40 dinars, try to get it to 25.

### Exercise B: Translate
1. Do you have this in blue?
2. This is too expensive. Is there something cheaper?
3. I want a bigger size.
4. This is better than that one.

### Exercise C: Describe items using colors and adjectives
1. A big red car
2. A small white house
3. Cheap black shoes
