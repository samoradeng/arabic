## Week 2 Comprehensive Test

### Part 1: Past Tense (20 points)

Conjugate these verbs in past tense for ALL 8 pronouns:
1. راح (went)
2. شاف (saw)

### Part 2: Translation to Jordanian Arabic (30 points)

1. Yesterday I went to the market and bought vegetables.
2. This jacket is too expensive. I'll give you 15 dinars.
3. In my opinion, Amman is better than Dubai.
4. Can I speak with Ahmad? — He's not here, call later.
5. My head hurts and I have a fever. I need medicine.
6. I'm going to travel to Petra next week, God willing.
7. Do you want to come with us? We're going to have dinner together.
8. I didn't understand. Can you repeat?
9. This is cheaper than that but that is better quality.
10. She was sick yesterday but today she's better.

### Part 3: Translation to English (20 points)

1. رحنا على السوق واشترينا كنادر. كانت غالية بس حلوة.
2. شو رأيك بالأكل الأردني؟ — والله أزكى أكل بالعالم.
3. آلو، مين معي؟ — أنا سامي. كيفك؟ بدي أسألك سؤال.
4. بوجعني ظهري من إمبارح. رحت على الدكتور وأعطاني دوا.
5. رح نتلاقى بكرة الساعة خمسة عند المول. ماشي؟

### Part 4: Situational Writing (30 points)

Write complete dialogues (6+ lines each) for these situations:

1. **Bargaining at a souk** — You want to buy a leather bag. The seller asks for 50 dinars.

2. **Calling a friend** — You call to plan going to a restaurant tomorrow evening.

3. **At the pharmacy** — You have a cold and need medicine.
