# Day 13: Future Tense & Making Plans

## Lesson Goals
- Express future actions using رح and بدي
- Make plans and appointments
- Use time expressions for the future
- Accept and decline invitations

---

## 1. Future Tense — المستقبل

Jordanian Arabic has two main ways to express the future:

### Method 1: رح (ra7) + verb — "going to"

This is the most common way. رح doesn't change — just add the subjunctive verb after it:

| Pronoun | Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning |
|---------|--------|----------------|---------|
| أنا | رح أروح | ra7 aroo7 | I'm going to go |
| إنتَ | رح تروح | ra7 troo7 | You're going to go |
| إنتِ | رح تروحي | ra7 troo7i | You're going to go |
| هو | رح يروح | ra7 yroo7 | He's going to go |
| هي | رح تروح | ra7 troo7 | She's going to go |
| إحنا | رح نروح | ra7 nroo7 | We're going to go |
| إنتو | رح تروحو | ra7 troo7u | You're going to go |
| هم | رح يروحو | ra7 yroo7u | They're going to go |

> **Note:** The verb after رح has NO بـ prefix. That's the subjunctive form.

### Method 2: بدي (biddi) + verb — "I want to / I'm going to"

- بدي أدرس بكرة — biddi adrus bukra — I'm going to study tomorrow
- بدنا نتغدى — bidna nitghadda — We're going to have lunch

### Examples

- رح أتصل فيك — ra7 atti9il feek — I'll call you
- رح نتلاقى الساعة خمسة — ra7 nitlaa2a is-saa3a khamse — We'll meet at 5
- رح أسافر على عمان — ra7 asaafir 3ala 3ammaan — I'm going to travel to Amman
- مش رح أقدر أيجي — mish ra7 a2dar aaji — I won't be able to come

### Negation

Just add **مش** before رح:
- مش رح أروح — mish ra7 aroo7 — I'm not going to go
- مش رح يوصل بالوقت — mish ra7 yoo9al bil-wa2t — He won't arrive on time

## 2. Future Time Expressions

| Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning |
|--------|----------------|---------|
| بكرة | bukra | tomorrow |
| بعد بكرة | ba3d bukra | day after tomorrow |
| الأسبوع الجاي | il-usboo3 ij-jaay | next week |
| الشهر الجاي | ish-shahar ij-jaay | next month |
| السنة الجاية | is-sane ij-jaaye | next year |
| بعد شوي | ba3d shwayy | in a little while |
| قريبا | 2areeban | soon |
| إن شاء الله | inshaallah | God willing (always add this) |

> **Cultural Note:** In Jordan, you almost ALWAYS say إن شاء الله when talking about the future. Not saying it can sound arrogant, as if you're certain about what will happen. Even atheists say it — it's cultural, not just religious.

## 3. Making Plans

### Inviting

| Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning |
|--------|----------------|---------|
| يلا نطلع | yalla nu6la3 | let's go out |
| بدك تيجي معنا؟ | biddak tiiji ma3na? | Do you want to come with us? |
| شو رأيك نتغدى سوا؟ | shu ra2yak nitghadda sawa? | How about we have lunch together? |
| عازمك على عشا | 3azmak 3ala 3asha | I'm inviting you to dinner |
| تعال عندنا | ta3aal 3indna | Come to our place |
| الليلة فاضي؟ | il-leile faa9i? | Are you free tonight? |

### Accepting

| Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning |
|--------|----------------|---------|
| أكيد! يلا! | akeed! yalla! | Of course! Let's go! |
| إن شاء الله | inshaallah | God willing (= yes) |
| بحب! | ba7ibb! | I'd love to! |
| ماشي | maashi | OK / sure |
| تسلم، يلا! | tislam, yalla! | Thanks, let's go! |

### Declining

| Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning |
|--------|----------------|---------|
| والله إني مشغول | wallah inni mashghool | I swear I'm busy |
| ما بقدر للأسف | maa ba2dar lil-asaf | I can't unfortunately |
| إن شاء الله مرة تانية | inshaallah marra taanye | God willing, another time |
| يا ريت بس عندي شغل | ya reit bass 3indi shughul | I wish, but I have work |
| يمكن، خليني أشوف | yimkin, khalleeni ashoof | Maybe, let me see |

> **Cultural Note:** When a Jordanian says إن شاء الله to a future plan, context matters:
> - **Enthusiastic inshaallah** = Yes, definitely
> - **Flat inshaallah** = Probably not going to happen
> - **بكرة إن شاء الله** (bukra inshaallah) = Famous phrase meaning "tomorrow maybe never"

## 4. Planning Dialogue

> — يا مارك، شو بدك تسوي يوم السبت؟
> — ya Mark, shu biddak tsawwi yoom is-sabt?
> — *Mark, what are you planning to do on Saturday?*

> — ما عندي شي. ليش؟
> — maa 3indi shi. leish?
> — *I have nothing. Why?*

> — رح أروح على البتراء مع صحابي. بدك تيجي معنا؟
> — ra7 aroo7 3al-batra ma3 s7aabi. biddak tiiji ma3na?
> — *I'm going to Petra with my friends. Want to come with us?*

> — والله؟ أكيد بدي! إمتى رح تطلعو؟
> — wallah? akeed biddi! imta ra7 tu6la3u?
> — *Really? Of course I want to! When are you leaving?*

> — رح نطلع الصبح بدري. الساعة ستة.
> — ra7 nu6la3 i9-9ub7 badri. is-saa3a sitte.
> — *We're leaving early morning. At 6.*

> — ستة بدري! بس ماشي. وين رح نتلاقى؟
> — sitte badri! bass maashi. wein ra7 nitlaa2a?
> — *6 is early! But OK. Where will we meet?*

> — بوصلك. أنا برن عليك الساعة خمسة و نص.
> — bwa99lak. ana brunn 3aleik is-saa3a khamse w nu99.
> — *I'll pick you up. I'll ring you at 5:30.*

> — تمام. رح أجهز حالي من الليلة. شكرا كتير!
> — tamaam. ra7 ajahiz 7aali min il-leile. shukran kteer!
> — *OK. I'll prepare myself tonight. Thanks a lot!*
