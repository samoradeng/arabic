# Day 10: Opinions, Agreeing & Disagreeing

## Lesson Goals
- Express opinions naturally
- Agree and disagree politely
- Use "I think," "In my opinion," "I feel"
- Discuss preferences

## 1. Expressing Opinions

| Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning |
|--------|----------------|---------|
| برأيي | bira2yi | in my opinion |

| أنا بحس إنه... | ana ba7iss innu... | I feel that... |
| أنا متأكد | ana mit2akkid | I'm sure |

## 5. Opinion Dialogue

> — شو رأيك بعمّان؟
> — shu ra2yak b-3ammaan?
> — *What do you think of Amman?*

> — والله، أنا بحب عمان كتير. الناس كتير طيبين.
> — wallah, ana ba7ibb 3ammaan kteer. in-naas kteer 6ayyibeen.
> — *Honestly, I love Amman a lot. The people are very kind.*

> — معك حق. بس الأزمة كتير صعبة.
> — ma3ak 7a22. bass il-azme kteer 9a3be.
> — *You're right. But the traffic is really bad.*

> — أكيد! بس أنا بفضل عمان على دبي. الصراحة عمان أحلى.
> — akeed! bass ana bfa99il 3ammaan 3ala dubai. i9-9araa7a 3ammaan a7la.
> — *Definitely! But I prefer Amman to Dubai. Honestly Amman is nicer.*

> — مش بالضرورة. دبي فيها أشياء كتير.
> — mish bi9-9aroora. dubai feeha ashyaa2 kteer.
> — *Not necessarily. Dubai has a lot of things.*

> — ممكن، بس الأردن فيها تاريخ. البتراء ووادي رم... مش معقول.
> — mumkin, bass il-urdun feeha taareekh. il-batra wwaadi rumm... mish ma32ool.
> — *Maybe, but Jordan has history. Petra and Wadi Rum... incredible.*

> — هيك بالظبط! بهالنقطة أنا معك مية بالمية.
> — heik bi6'-6ab6! b-han-nu26a ana ma3ak miyye bil-miyye.
> — *Exactly! On this point I agree 100%.*

## 6. Useful Opinion Structures

### "I think X is Y"
أنا بشوف إنه + subject + adjective

- أنا بشوف إنه الأكل الأردني أزكى أكل بالعالم
- *I think Jordanian food is the most delicious food in the world*

### "X should Y"
لازم + subject + verb (no بـ prefix)

- لازم تروح على البتراء — laazim troo7 3al-batra — You should go to Petra
- لازم نتعلم عربي — laazim nit3allam 3arabi — We should learn Arabic

### "I prefer X over Y"
بفضل X على Y

- بفضل الشاي على القهوة — bfa99il ish-shaay 3al-2ahwe — I prefer tea over coffee

## 7. Exercises

### Exercise A: Express your opinion on these topics
1. Jordanian food
2. Learning Arabic
3. Your city vs. Amman

### Exercise B: Agree or disagree with these statements
1. العربي أصعب لغة بالعالم (Arabic is the hardest language in the world)
2. المنسف أزكى أكل (Mansaf is the most delicious food)
3. الجو بالأردن حلو (The weather in Jordan is nice)

### Exercise C: Write a 6-line dialogue about a disagreement
Two people discussing whether it's better to learn Arabic from books or by talking to people.

---

### Answer Key

**Exercise B (example responses):**
1. مش بالضرورة. في لغات أصعب. بس العربي مش سهل — Not necessarily. There are harder languages. But Arabic isn't easy.
2. أكيد! المنسف أحلى أكل! — Definitely! Mansaf is the best food!
3. على حسب. بالصيف حار كتير — It depends. In summer it's very hot.
