# Jordanian Arabic Fluency Program - 21 Days

A structured, intensive program to reach conversational fluency in Jordanian/Levantine Arabic in 3 weeks. Designed for someone who can read Arabic script but needs dialect-specific vocabulary, grammar, and real-world conversation skills.

## Why Jordanian Arabic?

Jordanian Arabic is a Levantine dialect understood across Jordan, Palestine, and much of the Arab world. It's one of the most widely comprehended dialects thanks to Jordanian media and its similarity to Palestinian and Syrian Arabic.

**Key differences from MSA (Modern Standard Arabic):**
- The ق (qaf) is usually pronounced as a glottal stop **ء** (hamza) — e.g., قال → آل (*aal* = "he said")
- The ك (kaf) sometimes becomes **تش** (ch) in some words — e.g., كيف → كيف (*keef*)
- Verb forms are simplified compared to MSA
- Vocabulary borrows from Turkish, English, and French
- Word order is more flexible in casual speech

## Tips for Success

1. **Speak from Day 1** — Read every Arabic phrase out loud, every single time
2. **Shadow the dialogues** — Read them aloud mimicking natural speech rhythm
3. **Use the quiz tool daily** — Spaced repetition is the fastest path to retention
4. **Don't memorize, internalize** — Focus on patterns, not individual words
5. **Record yourself** — Compare your pronunciation to the transliterations
6. **Think in Arabic** — Start labeling objects around you in Arabic by Week 2
7. **Embrace mistakes** — Jordanians are incredibly warm to Arabic learners
